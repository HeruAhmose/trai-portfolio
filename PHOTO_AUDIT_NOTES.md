## Placement principles

- Use **real photographs** for trust, biography, community, and founder credibility.
- Use **concept renders / AI visuals** only in clearly visionary or systems-explainer contexts.
- Avoid crowding multiple images into the same section; one image should anchor one idea.
- Keep the home page restrained; place most imagery on dedicated pages rather than turning the homepage into a collage.
